## Scheme

Per the FCA, all seven are firms it lists as unauthorised, saying they appear
to offer or promote financial services without FCA authorisation. Three of the
new batch claim prestige City of London addresses (Bishopsgate, Canada Square,
Victoria Street), continuing the virtual-office pattern from the 06/11 batch,
and LORGANDIR-LEECH claims 375 Park Avenue in New York, a well-known office
tower. The two late-09/11 names were absent from yesterday's entry because the
FCA adds warnings through the day and a single morning read is partial.

## Typology

unauthorised firm; fake FX/crypto brand (AI+BTC naming); prestige-address dressing; clone firm (City Equities); invented compound-name branding

## Candidate detection signals (hypotheses)

- `[hypothesis]` Prestige-address dressing: a claimed Bishopsgate, Canary Wharf
  or Park Avenue address on a brand with no register presence is a review
  signal; these addresses recur across unrelated warnings.
- `[hypothesis]` Name-mash branding: names concatenating AI/crypto tokens
  ("aibtc") or invented compound surnames ("LORGANDIR-LEECH") cluster in this
  warning stream.
- `[hypothesis]` Read-lag control: comparing morning and evening snapshots of
  the list catches progressive publication; absence from one snapshot is not
  absence from the list.

## Notes

FCA warnings concern authorisation status; they do not by themselves prove
fraud. Addresses are what the firms claim. Phone numbers and emails from the
notices are not reproduced here. The City Equities warning page header still
shows a previous "last updated" date; the list's updated column shows
10/11/2026.
