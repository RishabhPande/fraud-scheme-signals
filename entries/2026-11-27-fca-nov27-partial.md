## Typology

unauthorised firm (eight new); domain-style firm names (six of eight); finance-style vocabulary in names ("securities", "fund", "capital", "wealth", "yield"); no or country-only postal address (four)

## Candidate detection signals (hypotheses)

- [hypothesis] Six of the eight names are domains. Domain-registration and site-age checks may be a useful review step. A domain-style name alone shows nothing about conduct.
- [hypothesis] Names that borrow established finance words ("securities", "wealth", "capital") may be worth checking against the FCA Firm Checker for similar authorised names. Similarity does not show impersonation.
- [hypothesis] Addresses that give only a country or region, or two addresses in different countries, may justify checking other identity evidence. The address pattern proves nothing about the firm.

## Limits

These are authorisation warnings, not findings that each firm committed fraud beyond what the FCA states. The FCA cautions that contact details in warnings can be incorrect or belong to someone else. Phone numbers, street addresses and email addresses in the notices are deliberately not reproduced. Signals above are review hypotheses only, not validated detection rules.
