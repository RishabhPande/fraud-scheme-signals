## Alerts

The FCA published six warnings on 23 Nov 2026, identifying the firms below as unauthorised and saying they may be targeting people in the UK. The notices report three UK addresses, one US address and two entries without a postal address; these are source-reported details, not independently verified locations or proof of fraud.

- **Kollips Trades:** [FCA notice](https://www.fca.org.uk/news/warnings/kollips-trades). The notice lists a London address.
- **BASICCOINMINERS:** [FCA notice](https://www.fca.org.uk/news/warnings/basiccoinminers). The notice lists a Borehamwood address.
- **Westline Fund:** [FCA notice](https://www.fca.org.uk/news/warnings/westline-fund). The notice lists an address in Castro Valley, California.
- **Future Wealth Capital Investment:** [FCA notice](https://www.fca.org.uk/news/warnings/future-wealth-capital-investment). The notice lists a Wokingham address.
- **Coinregis:** [FCA notice](https://www.fca.org.uk/news/warnings/coinregis). No postal address is shown in the notice.
- **Fiscate Limited:** [FCA notice](https://www.fca.org.uk/news/warnings/fiscate-limited). No postal address is shown in the notice.

## Typology

unauthorised firm; coin/mining naming; fund/wealth naming; cross-border address

## Candidate detection signals (hypotheses)

- [hypothesis] Coin/mining vocabulary can help group names for review, but cannot establish the product offered or any misconduct.
- [hypothesis] A non-UK address alongside an FCA warning may justify reviewing the claimed operating location and intended customer market. It does not establish a false address.
- [hypothesis] An omitted postal address may prompt a check of other identity evidence. The omission in a warning does not prove that the firm has no address or that its site is short-lived.
- [hypothesis] Fund/wealth vocabulary can help group investment-related names for review, but authorisation checks must use the relevant official record rather than the name alone.

## Limits

These are authorisation warnings, not findings that each named firm committed fraud. The FCA cautions that contact details in warnings can be incorrect or belong to someone else. Signals above are review hypotheses only, not validated detection rules.
