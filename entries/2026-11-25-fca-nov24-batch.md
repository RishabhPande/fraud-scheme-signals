## Alerts

The FCA published seven warnings on 24 Nov 2026. Five say the firm is unauthorised and may be targeting people in the UK. Two are clone-firm warnings, saying the named firm is not authorised but has contacted people pretending to be a genuine firm. Addresses below are source-reported details, not independently verified locations or proof of fraud.

- **VAULTACCESSGLOBAL:** [FCA notice](https://www.fca.org.uk/news/warnings/vaultaccessglobal). Unauthorised firm. The notice lists a London address and a website.
- **Empire 5G FX Pro Market:** [FCA notice](https://www.fca.org.uk/news/warnings/empire-5g-fx-pro-market). Unauthorised firm. The notice lists an address in Great Neck, New York.
- **WFM Holding / wfmltd.co.uk:** [FCA notice](https://www.fca.org.uk/news/warnings/wfm-holding-wfmltdcouk-clone-fca-authorised-firm). Clone of an FCA-authorised firm. The notice names WAY Fund Managers Limited as the genuine firm and says it has no connection with the clone.
- **PlusDirect24:** [FCA notice](https://www.fca.org.uk/news/warnings/plusdirect24-clone-fca-registered-firm). Clone of an FCA-registered firm. The notice names Carlatton Capital Limited as the genuine firm and says it has no connection with the clone.
- **goodivaulttrade.ltd:** [FCA notice](https://www.fca.org.uk/news/warnings/goodivaulttradeltd). Unauthorised firm. No postal address is shown. The notice's "other information" field points to a third-party review page.
- **argolislp.com:** [FCA notice](https://www.fca.org.uk/news/warnings/argolislpcom). Unauthorised firm. The notice lists an Edinburgh address and, like the entry above, points to a third-party review page.
- **housemining.energy:** [FCA notice](https://www.fca.org.uk/news/warnings/houseminingenergy). Unauthorised firm. The notice lists an address in Folkestone and points to a third-party review page.

Not included: Aurex Solid Global, first listed 25 Nov 2026. It goes in the next batch.

## Typology

unauthorised firm; clone firm (two); domain-style firm names (four); cross-border address; mining/vault naming

## Candidate detection signals (hypotheses)

- [hypothesis] Clone warnings name the genuine firm. Comparing a contact's claimed firm against the Firm Checker record, rather than against details the contact supplies, is a review step these notices support.
- [hypothesis] Firm names that are bare domains (for example argolislp.com) may be worth matching against domain-registration and website evidence during review. The name alone shows nothing about conduct.
- [hypothesis] A US address alongside an FCA warning may justify reviewing the claimed operating location and intended customer market. It does not establish a false address.
- [hypothesis] "Vault" and "mining" vocabulary can help group names for review, but cannot establish the product offered or any misconduct.

## Limits

These are authorisation and clone-firm warnings, not findings that each named firm committed fraud beyond what the FCA states. The FCA cautions that contact details in warnings can be incorrect or belong to someone else. Phone numbers and email addresses in the notices are deliberately not reproduced. Signals above are review hypotheses only, not validated detection rules.
