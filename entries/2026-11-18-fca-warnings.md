## Scheme

Per the FCA, global-reserve.co is a firm it lists as unauthorised, operating under a domain-only identity with no corporate name or address given. The update to the months-old Morizon Ltd warning continues the pattern of existing entries changing at any time; that operation pairs a UK residential-style address with a second, unrelated-looking property site (elixirheights.com) and YouTube promotion. Together the two items show the two poles of the current warning pattern: bare single-domain operations with no stated identity, and named firms spreading across several unrelated brands and channels.

## Typology

unauthorised firm; domain-only identity; multi-brand operation; social-media-led promotion; progressive-publication read lag

- [hypothesis] Financial-services offers from domains with no stated legal entity, company number or postal address, where the only contact is a generic support mailbox on the same domain.
- [hypothesis] A named firm marketing financial services while simultaneously operating unrelated consumer-facing brands (for example property or e-commerce) from the same contact details or address.
- [hypothesis] Months-old warnings that receive updates adding new domains or channels indicate an active operation still acquiring infrastructure; tracking a warning's added domains over time surfaces new fronts early.
