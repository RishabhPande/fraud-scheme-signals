# FCA Warning List: Bitrue (unauthorised crypto exchange promotion) and a deluxe-trade.top update

- **Date added:** 04/11/2026
- **Source:** FCA Warning List - https://www.fca.org.uk/consumers/warning-list-unauthorised-firms

## Alerts

- **Bitrue / www.bitrue.com** - unauthorised firm (new, 04/11/2026) - https://www.fca.org.uk/news/warnings/bitrue-wwwbitruecom
- **www.deluxe-trade.top** - existing warning updated (04/11/2026; first published 09/10/2025) - https://www.fca.org.uk/news/warnings/wwwdeluxe-tradetop

## Scheme

The FCA flags Bitrue as unauthorised: it says the firm appears to offer or promote financial services without FCA authorisation, and that people in the UK are a likely audience. Because Bitrue is not FCA-authorised, consumers dealing with it could not turn to the Financial Ombudsman Service or the FSCS. The notice lists a website plus X, YouTube and Telegram channels but no postal address. Unlike most recent entries, this one names an operating crypto exchange brand rather than a lookalike or clone, so it reflects the unregistered-promotion rule more than a typical scam pattern. Separately, the FCA updated its October 2025 warning on www.deluxe-trade.top, which lists an Ankara, Turkey address.

## Typology

unauthorised firm; unregistered financial promotion (crypto); social-channel promotion

## Candidate detection signals (hypotheses)

- `[hypothesis]` Register-absence check: a crypto exchange brand that does not appear on the FCA register or FCA crypto-registration list is worth review when UK customers pay or onboard through it.
- `[hypothesis]` Channel-based promotion: onboarding or payment references that trace to Telegram or social-channel links rather than a registered contact route.
- `[hypothesis]` Separate warned-but-not-fraudulent from warned-and-fraudulent: a regulator warning is about authorisation, not proof of fraud, so treat it as a review signal and not a block.

## Notes

The warning concerns authorisation status in the UK. It does not allege the exchange is fraudulent. The deluxe-trade.top update gives no detail of what changed in the notice. Email addresses are not reproduced here; see the linked notices.
