## Scheme

Per the FCA, all six are clone firms: operations that copy the details of genuine authorised or registered firms so their pitch looks legitimate. The batch spans retail sectors - currency exchange, mortgages, insurance, claims management - and uses the usual techniques: near-identical domains, borrowed firm names, and fake UK office addresses. Car Insur goes a step further by quoting the genuine firm's Firm Reference Number.

## Typology

clone firm; lookalike domain; brand impersonation; stolen registration details; fake prestige address; cross-sector batch

## Candidate detection signals (hypotheses)

- [hypothesis] Domain registrations clustered in time that sit one edit away from authorised-firm domains (edit-distance or confusable-character match against the FCA register) flag clone infrastructure early.
- [hypothesis] Marketing or onboarding text quoting a Firm Reference Number that resolves to a different firm name or contact set than the one presented.
- [hypothesis] Claimed office addresses on well-known commercial streets with no matching occupant in public tenant or postal records.
- [hypothesis] The same website template or contact backend reused across unrelated financial products (FX, mortgages, insurance).
