# FCA Warning List: Tractus Trade, Argentum Trade and Sky Bing Bank

- **Date added:** 05/11/2026
- **Source:** FCA Warning List - https://www.fca.org.uk/consumers/warning-list-unauthorised-firms

## Alerts

- **Tractus Trade** (tractustrade.pro) - unauthorised firm, first published 05/11/2026 - https://www.fca.org.uk/news/warnings/tractus-trade
- **Argentum Trade** (argentumtrade.pro) - unauthorised firm, first published 05/11/2026 - https://www.fca.org.uk/news/warnings/argentum-trade
- **Sky Bing Bank** (skybingbank.online) - unauthorised firm, first published 05/11/2026 - https://www.fca.org.uk/news/warnings/sky-bing-bank

## Scheme

The FCA lists all three as unauthorised firms it says are offering financial services without authorisation. Two are "-trade" brands on .pro domains and one presents as a bank on a .online domain. Sky Bing Bank gives a London, Westminster address (SW1A 1AA), a central-London postcode that is not evidence of a real office. The notices give no detail on what victims are offered.

## Typology

unauthorised firm; lookalike trading platform; fake bank; cheap-TLD domains

## Candidate detection signals (hypotheses)

- `[hypothesis]` Domain pattern: new "<name>trade" or "<name>bank" sites on .pro, .online or .top TLDs that ask for deposits, especially when two such brands appear in the same week.
- `[hypothesis]` Name-versus-register check: a "bank" name with no match on the FCA or PRA register is worth review before onboarding or payout.
- `[hypothesis]` Address plausibility: a prestige central-London address on a site with no register entry is a weak signal alone. Combine with domain age and payment route.

## Notes

An FCA warning concerns authorisation status. It does not by itself prove fraud. Phone numbers and emails from the notices are not reproduced here; see the linked pages.
