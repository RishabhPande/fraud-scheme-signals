## Scheme

Per the FCA, ROCKWELL INTERNATIONAL PARTNERS is a firm it lists as unauthorised, presenting a New York corporate identity and website while potentially targeting people in the UK. The same-day updates to three older warnings continue the pattern that existing entries change at any time: 501FX's entry now covers a third domain, @MarkOwensConcepts shows an operation run almost entirely through mainstream social-media accounts rather than a conventional site, and Whitmore-Calloway's entry refreshes another US-addressed outfit. Three of the four warnings in this batch present US addresses while the FCA warns they may be targeting the UK.
